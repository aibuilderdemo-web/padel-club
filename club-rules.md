# Padel Club Navigli: club rules

1. Opening hours. Courts are open every day from 8:00 to 23:00. The last booking starts at 21:30.
2. Booking. A booking lasts 90 minutes. Book in the app: members up to 7 days ahead, guests up to 2 days ahead.
3. Members and guests. Members pay €25 a month and book at member prices. Guests book without an account and pay at reception.
4. Guests of members. A member can bring one guest per booking. The guest pays a €10 fee at reception.
5. Cancelling. Cancel in the app up to 24 hours before the start for a full refund. Later cancellations are not refunded.
6. Rain. Courts 1 and 2 are indoor. If it rains on courts 3 or 4, we move you to an indoor court if one is free. If none is free, you get a refund.
7. No-shows. Three no-shows in one month pause your bookings for 14 days.
8. Arriving. Arrive 10 minutes early. Arriving late doesn't extend your booking.
9. Shoes. Padel or tennis shoes with clean soles only. No running shoes with deep grip, no black soles.
10. Lights. Court lights are included in the price of every evening booking.
11. Rackets and balls. Rent a racket at reception for €3. Balls are sold at reception.
12. Lessons. Lessons with a club coach are booked at reception, not in the app.
13. Outside coaches. Coaches who don't work for the club can't teach on our courts.
14. Minors. Players under 16 play only with an adult member on the same court.
15. Changing rooms. Changing rooms and showers are free for players with a booking.
16. Food and drink. Only water bottles on court. The bar is next to reception.
17. Dogs. Dogs on a lead are welcome in the bar area, never on court.
18. Lockers. Lockers are free. Bring your own padlock.
19. Lost property. Reception keeps lost items for 30 days.
20. Parking. Free bike parking at the entrance. The club has no car park.
21. Behaviour. Shouting at other players, swearing, or hitting the glass on purpose can lead to a ban.
22. Damage. Players pay for damage they cause on purpose.
23. Tournaments. On tournament days some courts are closed. The app shows them as not free.
24. Ending a membership. Members end their membership in the app. It stops at the end of the month already paid.
25. Anything else. For anything these rules don't cover, ask at reception or call the club.
